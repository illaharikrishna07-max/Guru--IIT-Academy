About Guru IIT Academy

Guru IIT Academy focuses on IIT-JEE and foundation-level preparation for students in Classes 8–12. Based in Gurramguda, Hyderabad, the academy offers long-term and short-term programs designed around focused preparation.

Students build their preparation through concept-based learning, problem-solving practice and academic guidance—whether they are beginning with foundation studies or preparing for IIT-JEE.
